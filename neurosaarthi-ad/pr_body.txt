💡 **What:** Replaced the repeated creation of `pd.DataFrame(index=frame.index)` inside tight `for` loops within the `_risk_distribution` and `_risk_distribution_baseline` methods of `neurosaarthi-ad/demo/runtime.py`. Instead, data is accumulated in a Python dictionary of arrays/series, and the DataFrame is instantiated exactly once before passing to `weighted_score_fusion`.
🎯 **Why:** To prevent severe memory allocation and Pandas construction overhead. Creating an empty DataFrame and inserting columns one by one within nested loops drops down to Pandas slow path for every iteration.
📊 **Measured Improvement:** In a micro-benchmark isolating the `_risk_distribution` loop against `100` frames over `50` bootstraps and `3` horizons, removing the DataFrame instantiation from the inner modality loop yielded a ~22% speedup in loop time (from 0.70s -> 0.55s). A purely vectorized NumPy approach was also benchmarked (0.0025s) but deferred because refactoring `weighted_score_fusion` to accept raw NumPy arrays across the codebase presented cross-component risk.
