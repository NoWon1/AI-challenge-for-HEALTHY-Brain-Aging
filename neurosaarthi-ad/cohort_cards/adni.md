# ADNI Cohort Card

## Best Use

Biomarker-rich longitudinal Alzheimer's disease benchmarking across clinical, cognitive, MRI/PET, biofluid, and genomics data.

## Do Not Use For

India-specific calibration, population prevalence estimation, or OCT/OCTA-first modelling.

## Starter Endpoints

- Cognitively unimpaired to MCI.
- MCI to dementia/AD.
- CDR-SB or MMSE progression over fixed horizons.
