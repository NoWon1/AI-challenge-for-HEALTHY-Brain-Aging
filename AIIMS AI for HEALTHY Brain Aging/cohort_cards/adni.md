# ADNI Cohort Card

## Best Use

Biomarker-rich longitudinal Alzheimer's disease (AD) benchmarking across clinical, cognitive, MRI/PET, biofluid, and genomics data.

## Do Not Use For

- Indian population-specific calibration.
- Population prevalence estimation.
- OCT/OCTA-first modelling.

## Starter Endpoints

- Cognitively unimpaired to MCI [Mild Cognitive Impairment].
- MCI to dementia/AD [Alzheimer's Disease].
- CDR-SB [Clinical Dementia Rating Scale Sum of Boxes] or MMSE [Mini-Mental State Examination] progression over fixed horizons.
