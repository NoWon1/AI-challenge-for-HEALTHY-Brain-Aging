# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free research community. Treat contributors, study communities, annotators, and data stewards with dignity. Avoid stigmatising language or biological essentialism, especially in rural/urban, ancestry, education, sex, disability, or cognitive-status analyses.

Scientific disagreement should focus on evidence, methods, uncertainty, and reproducibility. Privacy violations, fabrication, metric cherry-picking, hidden leakage, discriminatory conduct, and exaggerated clinical claims are unacceptable. Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers or institutional contact.
